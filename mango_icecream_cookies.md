# mango_icecream_cookies
inspired my GBO, Geppo season

for two,
takes about 20 minutes, cools 4 hours
## ingredients
- 600 g mango, semifrozen.
- 75 g sugar
- 250 ml full fat cream
- leaves of mint
- digestive cookies
  
## recipe
1. rest or microwave frozen mango to about -5°C
2. blend the mango and mint, add cream, then sugar 
3. pour in the icemaker and "manteca" for 20 minutes
4. freeze two/four hours
5. pick a disc slightly smaller the the cookies
6. shape icecream to disc
7. slightly press cookies top and bottom
8. freeze again
9. take out about 10 minutes bofore serving
